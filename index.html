Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UK VAT rate?
The standard UK VAT rate is 20%. The reduced rate is 5% (e.g. energy, children's car seats) and zero rate is 0% (e.g. food, children's clothing).
How do I remove VAT?
Divide the VAT-inclusive amount by 1.2 (for 20% VAT) to get the net amount. Our calculator does this automatically.
